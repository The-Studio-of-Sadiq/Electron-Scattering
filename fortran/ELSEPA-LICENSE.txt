readme.txt in this folder, and the atomic data tables in ../data (z_NNN.den, z_NNN.dfs), are the work of

  Francesc Salvat, Aleksander Jablonski and Cedric J. Powell,
  "ELSEPA - Dirac partial-wave calculation of elastic scattering of electrons and positrons by
  atoms, positive ions and molecules (New Version Announcement)",
  Comput. Phys. Commun. 261 (2021) 107704, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2020.107704
  Original description: Comput. Phys. Commun. 165 (2005) 157-190, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2004.09.006
  RADIAL: F. Salvat, J.M. Fernandez-Varea, Comput. Phys. Commun. 240 (2019) 165-177,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2019.02.011

The files in this folder are unmodified copies of the distributed files. For the WebAssembly build,
wasm/f90to77.py generates, at build time, a Fortran 77 copy in which the module CONSTANTS (six
PARAMETER constants in radial.f) is written as an INCLUDE of the same constants, because the f2c
translator does not accept Fortran 90 modules. No other change is made.

The earlier (2005) version of ELSEPA is no longer distributed with this software, at the request of
its authors.
